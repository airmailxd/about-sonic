Key concepts, chapter by chapter

Each chapter of System Design Interview (Alex Xu, Vol. 1 & 2) is covered in six parts:

  • The big picture: what problem the chapter solves and why the design looks the way it does.
  • Key concepts: the terms you should be able to define.
  • How it works: the request or data flow, step by step.
  • Trade-offs: the options, with pros and cons.
  • Pitfalls & follow-up questions: what interviewers tend to probe.
  • Acronyms, plus a collapsed "How this relates to SONiC" note. Try to make the connection yourself before you open it.
